About Practiq Academy

Learn Skills. Build Projects. Create Possibilities.

Practiq Academy is an e-learning platform being built to help students and professionals develop practical skills across artificial intelligence, analytics, marketing and management. Our focus is on connecting concepts with real-world applications through guided learning, hands-on activities and projects.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make practical, technology-enabled learning more accessible. We aim to help learners understand new concepts, apply their knowledge, solve problems and develop projects that demonstrate their skills.

What We Focus On

Artificial Intelligence & ML

Explore AI concepts, responsible AI use, machine learning fundamentals and practical applications.

Business Analytics

Learn to interpret data, identify patterns and communicate insights that support informed decisions.

Marketing

Understand customers, digital marketing, campaign analysis and the role of data and AI in marketing.

Management

Explore business fundamentals, strategic thinking, decision-making and technology-enabled management.

How We Approach Learning

Learn the Concepts

Build a foundation through clear explanations and structured learning activities.

Apply Your Knowledge

Work through practical exercises, examples and business or technology problems.

Build Projects

Practise your skills by developing projects and reflecting on what you have learned.

Meet the Founder

Rishabh Parmar

Practiq Academy is founded by Rishabh Parmar, whose interests span management, marketing, artificial intelligence, machine learning and analytics. His vision is to connect business understanding with technology and practical implementation, helping learners explore how these disciplines work together.

Through Practiq Academy, the goal is to create a learning environment where curiosity leads to practice, practice leads to projects, and projects help learners build confidence.
