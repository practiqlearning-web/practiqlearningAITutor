About the Programme

Turn Business Data into Useful Insights

Organisations collect information about sales, customers, operations, marketing and financial performance. The challenge is to turn that information into sound business decisions.

AI can assist with preparing data, exploring patterns, generating code or formulas, creating summaries and developing analytical workflows. But useful analysis also requires clear questions, appropriate methods, validation and business understanding.

This programme connects those skills through a practical, business-oriented approach to analytics.

What You Will Work Towards

  • Frame business problems as analytical questions.
  • Understand and prepare structured datasets.
  • Explore data using descriptive statistics and summaries.
  • Create charts, visualisations and dashboards.
  • Interpret patterns, trends and performance indicators.
  • Explore introductory forecasting and predictive models.
  • Validate AI-generated calculations and explanations.
  • Communicate insights through a business report.

60-Day Learning Roadmap

Explore the AI Analytics Curriculum

Eight proposed modules move from foundational data skills towards business analysis, predictive methods and a capstone. Confirm the final session schedule before enrolment.

Days 1–7

Module 1: Foundations of AI & Business Analytics

Understand analytics, AI and the role of data in business decision-making.

  • Descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics
  • Business questions and analytical problem framing
  • Structured data, variables and data types
  • AI capabilities, limitations and verification
Days 8–15

Module 2: Data Preparation & Spreadsheet Analysis

Learn how to inspect, organise and prepare data for reliable analysis.

  • Data types, tables and basic data quality checks
  • Missing values, duplicates and inconsistent entries
  • Spreadsheet formulas and conditional calculations
  • AI-assisted formula generation and troubleshooting
  • Documenting data preparation decisions
Days 16–23

Module 3: Exploratory & Descriptive Analytics

Examine what a dataset contains and identify patterns that deserve further investigation.

  • Counts, percentages, averages and distributions
  • Comparisons across products, regions or customer groups
  • Trend analysis and outlier identification
  • Correlation versus causation
  • AI-assisted interpretation with calculation checks
Days 24–30

Module 4: Data Visualisation & Dashboards

Present analytical findings in a clear and useful format for business audiences.

  • Choosing appropriate charts and graphs
  • Data storytelling and visual communication
  • Key performance indicators and scorecards
  • Dashboard layout and information hierarchy
  • Interpreting dashboard findings
Days 31–38

Module 5: Business Intelligence & Performance Analysis

Connect data analysis with business performance monitoring and operational questions.

  • Sales, revenue, cost and customer metrics
  • Actual performance versus targets
  • Variance and trend analysis
  • Segment and product performance comparisons
  • Turning findings into business recommendations
Days 39–45

Module 6: Predictive Analytics & Forecasting

Explore how historical data can be used to estimate future outcomes and support planning.

  • Prediction problems and target variables
  • Forecasting concepts and trend-based projections
  • Introduction to regression and classification
  • Training and testing concepts
  • Prediction errors, limitations and uncertainty
Days 46–52

Module 7: AI-Assisted Insights & Decision Support

Use AI to assist in explaining findings and structuring evidence-based recommendations.

  • AI-assisted analytical questions and workflows
  • Summarising tables, charts and results
  • Scenario comparison and decision frameworks
  • Checking calculations, sources and interpretations
  • Writing concise analytical reports
Days 53–60

Module 8: Business Analytics Capstone

Apply the programme's concepts to a selected business dataset and communicate the results.

  • Define a business problem and analysis objectives
  • Prepare and explore the dataset
  • Identify relevant trends and performance measures
  • Develop charts or a dashboard
  • Prepare a report with findings and recommendations
  • Present limitations and suggested next steps

Tools & Techniques

Understand the Method, Then Use the Tools

AI can assist with analysis, but the quality of the result depends on data quality, correct calculations and suitable methods. The programme is designed to develop that understanding.

Tool selection should match the learner's level, the available software and the intended project. Paid subscriptions are not included unless explicitly stated.
