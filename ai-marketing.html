About the Programme

Marketing Knowledge Meets Artificial Intelligence

Marketing is more than creating posts or running advertisements. It involves understanding people, researching markets, building brands, managing customer relationships and making strategic decisions.

This programme explores how AI tools and analytical methods can support each of these activities. The emphasis is on applying marketing concepts, asking better questions, interpreting outputs and making informed decisions—not simply generating content.

What You Can Work Towards

  • Use AI tools to support marketing research and planning.
  • Develop audience profiles and customer personas.
  • Plan content and digital marketing campaigns.
  • Explore brand positioning and messaging.
  • Interpret customer feedback and marketing performance.
  • Build a portfolio project around a marketing problem.
  • Recognise limitations, bias and responsible uses of AI.

60-Day Learning Roadmap

Explore the Curriculum

A proposed learning structure moving from marketing foundations to applied AI workflows and a final project. The precise weekly schedule can be finalised before the cohort begins.

Days 1–7

Module 1: Introduction to AI in Marketing

Understand AI, generative AI and their applications across marketing functions.

  • AI, machine learning and generative AI fundamentals
  • Prompt design for marketing tasks
  • Evaluating AI responses and checking accuracy
  • Responsible use of customer and business data
Days 8–15

Module 2: Consumer Behaviour & Customer Insights

Explore how AI can support understanding customer needs, motivations and buying behaviour.

  • Consumer needs, motivations and purchase journeys
  • Customer personas and audience profiles
  • Segmentation and customer journey mapping
  • Analysing sample reviews and customer feedback
Days 16–23

Module 3: AI-Powered Digital Marketing

Explore how AI can assist with digital campaign planning and channel-specific marketing activities.

  • Digital marketing channels and campaign objectives
  • Content calendars and campaign ideation
  • SEO research and search-intent exploration
  • Social media planning and audience engagement
  • Campaign KPIs and performance measurement
Days 24–30

Module 4: AI for Branding & Content Strategy

Examine how AI can support brand research, communication and consistent messaging.

  • Brand identity, positioning and differentiation
  • Brand voice and messaging frameworks
  • Content briefs and creative ideation
  • Reviewing AI-generated copy for quality and originality
Days 31–38

Module 5: AI-Assisted Market Research

Use AI to support research planning, information organisation and preliminary interpretation.

  • Research objectives and questionnaire planning
  • Competitor and market research frameworks
  • Organising survey responses and open-ended feedback
  • Basic sentiment and theme analysis
  • Source verification and research limitations
Days 39–45

Module 6: AI & Customer Relationship Management

Explore how customer data and AI-supported workflows may help businesses manage customer relationships.

  • CRM concepts and customer lifecycle management
  • Customer segmentation and personalisation
  • Customer support and response workflows
  • Retention, loyalty and engagement metrics
  • Privacy-aware customer data practices
Days 46–52

Module 7: AI-Enabled Marketing Strategy

Connect customer understanding, brand positioning, channels and business objectives into a coherent strategy.

  • Market positioning and value propositions
  • Marketing objectives, segmentation and targeting
  • AI-assisted campaign planning
  • Budget and KPI considerations
  • Interpreting results and recommending improvements
Days 53–60

Module 8: Capstone Project & Presentation

Bring the concepts together in a practical marketing challenge using a sample business or a suitable project brief.

  • Define the marketing problem and target audience
  • Conduct customer and competitor research
  • Develop a brand or campaign strategy
  • Define KPIs and an evaluation approach
  • Present recommendations with supporting evidence
